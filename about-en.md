# About the Author

**Nebula Walker** (星忘塵) — born in Hong Kong, based in Taiwan. Independent writer and industry analyst.

An engineer by training, with a career spanning three continents: R&D labs in Australia, embedded systems engineering (ARM, RTOS, driver development), technical marketing and applications engineering across Asia-Pacific, and corporate strategy consulting for acquisition restructuring — across several multinationals. Witnessed firsthand how institutions grind people down through engineered attrition, balance-sheet cleanup, and systems that need no villains to do damage.

After leaving the corporate world, turned to long-form writing.

No courses to sell. No anxiety to peddle. No disguised endorsements. What I write is structure — how capital strips an industry to the bone, how narrative covers power, and how AI tears the covering off.

Mythogen Engine is my independent publishing platform. Everything is free to read, with no advertising budget. Every share is a vote.
