# Where Does the Chaos Go? The Gap Between Promise and Reality Never Disappears; It Only Moves

@Mythogen Engine · Nov 23, 2026

The gap between what is promised and what is real never disappears; it only moves: first into Excel, then into the organization where it settles as legacy, paid for by the monopoly margin, and finally recorded against individuals. Four articles follow that path; a side article asks what you can choose when you are inside it and have no voice.

This series extends [*Who's Collecting at the Door*](../../books/who-collects/INFO_PAGE.md). That book is about the moment veto power is taken away; this series is about what happens over the following decade.

## Reading Order

1. [You Can't Have Both Freedom and Lossless Integration](./01-freedom-and-lossless-integration.md): the gap lands first in Excel
2. [The Monster Becomes a Seat](./02-the-monster-becomes-a-seat.md): the gap settles in the organization as legacy
3. [Each Time the Problem Gets Worse, Someone Gets Promoted](./03-problem-worse-person-promoted.md): the people who guard the seat, and the monopoly margin that pays for it all
4. [The Last Field Never Names the Company](./04-the-last-field-never-names-the-company.md): the bill is finally recorded against individuals

Side article. [No One Comes Forward, and It Isn't for Lack of a Case](./05-side-no-one-comes-forward.md): what you can choose when you are inside it and have no voice
